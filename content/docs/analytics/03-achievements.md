# Achievements
This is located in the analytics page, ion the right side of the appbar, the trophy icon one. Click it to be redirected to the achievements page. Here, you can see what you achieve in the times you started selling and tracking your items

## Items Sold
It will tell you the level of item you already sold and your next goal

## Total Profit
This is pretty much self explanatory, same stuff with items sold

## Daily Sale Streak
This shows your streak, like continuous day with sold items. If you did a 30 days streak, well then good for your business

## Monthly Sale Streak
same same but different with daily sale, this just monthly streak. So if you have miss a month, maybe that's the matter to think the career path you choose. But hey, if you're going through something, just take your time and bounce hard
