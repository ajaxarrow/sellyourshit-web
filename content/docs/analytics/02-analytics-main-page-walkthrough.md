# Analytics Main Page Walkthrough
The Analytics Main page is just a bunch of widgets and stats cards that tell you how your business is doing — if you're good for the month, or if you should leave this venture and go start a farm.
Note: All of these sections are in accordion mode, so you can collapse them if you want.

## Overview
Overview is grouped into 6 main stats cards:

- **Total Sales** — Every dollar that's collected from the sold price.
- **Capital** — This is the accumulation of every out-of-pocket expense you spent, like the sum of all the purchase prices. If you turned on the closet feature, the closet garments' purchase price is excluded here.
- **Profit** — Your revenue minus cost of goods sold. If you turned on the closet feature, all of the sold amount of closet garments is included here.
- **Sell-Through** — Share of stock sold, like the percentage of your total stock that's been sold.
- **Average Days To Sell** — I guess this is pretty self-explanatory.
- **Repeat Customer** — If you're still reading this and wondering what "repeat customer" means, you need to find another venture.

- **Capital from Closet** — As I've said, this is just an indicator of what you spent on your closet items. It's not included in the total capital listed above.
- **Profit from Closet** — This is your total revenue from your decluttered garments. Full price counts as profit since no cost is counted here, since these are your decluttered things.

- **Issue Sell-Through** — This just shows the percentage of the items you sell with issues that are sold. This card is clickable, so click it for more details.
- **No Issue Sell-Through** — This is just the comparison percentage — the sell-through of items with no issues.

## Sales Over Time

### Tabs
This is your total sales over time. The first tab refers to the date range, so you can compare by day, week, month, and year — default is month. You can also modify the chart output to show total sales in revenue or total sales in clothes sold.

### Best Periods
Best Period is different from the charts, but it's dependent on the time selected — if it's year, then it displays the top 5 years. This just shows your total sales and your best-performing period. The period is clickable, so you can see all the garments sold in that time period.

## Brand and Types
So in here, you'll be presented with the top brands or types of garments you have in a certain category. There are two layers of tabs here — the first is to view the top sold items or current stock per item, and the second layer of tabs is to show by brand or type.

### SOLD
This has 2 sections — the first is the top brands/types sold based on revenue, and the second based on quantity sold. This will help you determine your top-selling brands/types.

### Current Stock
This just shows you the top brands/types you have with the most current stock, both in quantity and in price. Note that all list items here are clickable.

## Channel Performance
This will show you how your channels perform. Channel is the term we use for the platform you sold this specific item on.
These cards are also clickable. You can also filter by range — today, week, month, or all.

## Top Customers
Maybe you record your customers, maybe not. This might be helpful to track your repeat customers and those who support your business.

## Top Performers
This just shows the item/transaction with the best profit percentage, and the most money you earned from an item or a transaction. It displays largest profit, largest sale, and best margin. It also separates closet items into their own list — for example, in largest profit, closet items don't include capital, so the whole sold amount goes straight to profit.
