# Exporting your Data
I don't think this is really something small businesses do, but for large scale, or if you just like doing it, you can export your analytics data and inventory in Excel or PDF. Look, if our charts aren't enough and you need a more detailed spreadsheet for analytics, that's okay — you'll hurt our dev feelings, but that's alright. But if you need to export your inventory to Excel, bro, why? We literally built this stuff for you so you can ditch using a generic spreadsheet. But because we want to spoil our audience, you can still export it.

## What Formats Can You Export Your Data In?
**.xlsx** or **.pdf**. That's the only option — no JSON for now. Maybe that could be something we do for monetization. Hey, blame the economy. We can't just give you everything for free. These devs gotta eat too.

### What Data Can You Export?
Inventory, sold transactions, analytics summary, and capital movements. Basically any important stuff here.

### Date Range
You can customize which month you want to export, or you can also choose a specific date range.
