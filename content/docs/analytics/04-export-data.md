# Exporting your Data
I think this is not really something that small business do but for large scale or if you just like doing it, you can export your analytics data and inventory in excel or pdf. Look, if our charts not enough and you need more detailed spreadsheet for analytics, that's okay. You will hurt our dev feelings but that's alright. But if you need to export your inventory to excel, bro why? We literally built this stuff for you so you can ditch using generic spreadsheet. But because we want to spoil our audience, you can still export it

## What Formats You Can Export Your Data?
.xlsx or .pdf. That's the only option, no json for now. Maybe that could be something we can do for monetization. Hey blame the economy. We cannot just give all stuff for you for free. These devs gotta eat too

### What data you can export?
Inventory, sold transactions, analytics summary and capital movements. Basically any important stuff here

### Date Range
You can customize what month yoou want to export or you can also choose by a specific date range
