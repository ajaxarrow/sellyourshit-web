# Selling Items

Look, there are two ways to sell an item in sellyoshit. Let's start
with the easy, straightforward one.

## Via Details Page

### Choosing an Item

1. Go to Inventory and tap the item you want to mark as sold.
2. On the details page, tap **Mark as Sold**. See
   [Item Details](./03-item-details-page.md#mark-as-sold-button) for
   where that button lives. It's one big, highlighted button — if you
   still can't find it, that's on you, not our frontend developer.

### Main Details

Once you tap the button, a modal overlay pops up asking for:

- **Sold Price** — the actual price you sold it for.
- **Channel** — where you sold it. Carousell and Facebook Page are the
  defaults, and you can add more from there or manage them in Settings.
- **Sold Date** — check the box if it sold today, or uncheck it and
  pick the actual date if you're the type to forget logging things for
  a week.
- **Customer** — not a big deal, but this is a reselling business, and
  repeat customers are worth tracking. Manage your customer list in
  Settings.

### Freebie Items

Sometimes you throw in a freebie to make up for your own
incompetence — like selling an item with an issue and hoping a freebie
smooths it over. When that's you, tap **Add Freebie** and pick an item
to give away, even though you really don't want to.

## Via the Select Icon in Inventory

### Selecting Items

1. Tap the **checkbox icon** in the upper-right of the Inventory page.
2. Pick out the items you want to select. You can still search or
   filter while doing this — see
   [Filtering and Searching](./05-filtering-and-searching.md) — so it's
   easier to track down exactly what you sold.

Yes, we built it this way to make your life easier. So thank us.
Please.

### Bulk Select

This lets you sell multiple items to one customer in a single
transaction — say a customer bought 5 items in one go. Select all of
them, then tap **Sell N Items**.

### Bundle Total Feature

By default, you enter a selling price per item. But sometimes there's
no per-item price — a customer just says "5 for 500" and calls it a
day. That's what the **Bundle Total** option is for: enter the total,
and the app splits it across the items for you. You don't need to work
out the individual sold price yourself, you lazy bastard.

## Confirm Sale

Once every detail's filled in, tap **Confirm** and you're done.

### Undo Sale

Undo a sale by opening the sold item and tapping **Undo Sale** — see
[Item Details](./03-item-details-page.md#undo-sale-button) for exactly
what that does. Heads up: this only undoes that one garment. If it was
part of a bulk transaction, the rest of the sale stays intact. Undoing
an entire transaction in one shot isn't covered in these docs yet.
