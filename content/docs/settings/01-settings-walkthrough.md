# Settings Walkthrough
Notice this page said walkthrough and not overview. Yeah, I'm tired of creating these docs. I think I could fit all of the content in one page, so let's see how this one goes.

## Profile
Here you can edit the things you input during onboarding.

### Track Closet Items
Yes, this is the page — it's pretty easy, obviously. You just need to check the only **checkbox** on this page to track your closet items. See [Closet vs. Sourced Items](../inventory/01-inventory-overview.md#closet-vs-sourced-items) for how this shows up once it's tracked.

## Appearance
Again, pretty straightforward stuff. I think I spent more time typing this than I ever would if I just explained what this does — that pretty much sums up how easy this really is.

## Manage List
These items are the things that appear across the application: **Brands**, **Garment Types**, **Sales Channels**, and **Customers**. They all have the same logic — just click one, add some stuff, and you can edit the text or delete it. There are some pre-added fields, like in Brands and Types, and you can edit all of them.

## Data
So sellyoshit is a fully offline application (except when you submit a ticket or view the docs, both of which are discussed next). The founder doesn't have the budget to make this a cloud-based app, so all the data lives on your phone. If you erase the data, bye-bye inventory and all your input data. But thankfully, we do have solutions for that.

### Backup & Restore
1. Click this item and you'll be redirected to another page.
2. From there, you'll see:
   - **Backup Now** — backs up all your data, from profile, inventory, and sales, including the images. It'll be saved as a JSON file into your selected app location.
   - **Restore from backup** — restores your data from a selected JSON backup file.

### Reset Data
I don't know the reason for you resetting your data — you could've just gone to settings and cleared storage — but in here you can reset your data. Sellyoshit gives you a selection of what items to reset, tho.
