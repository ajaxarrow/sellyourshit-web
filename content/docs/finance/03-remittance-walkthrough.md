# Remittance Overview
This is sort of a miscellaneous kind of feature but hey, if you want to track the profit that you get in the app, this is the right feature for you

## Stats Cards
Total Profit: This is your total profit
Total Remitted: This is the amount you remitt. Just think of it like withdrawing in the bank. Profit is money in bank and you need to reap the fruit from the seed you once planted. Shit goes poetic for a sec right there.
Available to remit: these are the stuff you can remit

## Remittance Timeline
This is the timeline of how many times you remit, so it jsut show the date you remitted, the amopunt and the reason

## How to Remit
