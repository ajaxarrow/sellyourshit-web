# Get Started

sellyoshit is an inventory and sales tracker for resellers. Here's how to get it installed, plus answers to the questions people usually ask before or right after.

## How to Download

### Option 1: From the Website
Go to the [homepage](/) and click **Download for Android**.

### Option 2: Direct Download
Skip the extra step — [download the APK directly](/api/download).

## FAQs

### What platforms does sellyoshit run on?
Android only, for now. There's no iOS or web version.

### What can sellyoshit actually do?
Inventory management, sales tracking, exporting, and remittance tracking. That's the full feature set right now.

### Do I need an internet connection to use it?
No. sellyoshit runs fully offline. The only times you need a connection are submitting a support ticket or opening the docs from inside the app.

### Where is my data stored?
On your phone, locally. There's no cloud sync — nothing leaves your device unless you export or back it up yourself.

### What happens if I lose my phone or delete the app?
Whatever wasn't backed up is gone. Use **Backup Now** in Settings to save your data, including images, as a JSON file, and **Restore from backup** to bring it back on a new device.

### Can I export my data?
Yes — inventory, sold transactions, analytics summary, and capital movements, as .xlsx or .pdf.

### Is sellyoshit free?
Yes, it's free to download and use.

### Can I use it on more than one device at once?
Not automatically. Each install keeps its own local data, so you'd need to back up on one device and restore on the other.

### How do I report a bug or ask a question?
Use **Submit a Ticket** in Settings, or go to the [contact page](/contact).

That covers the basics. The rest of the docs go deeper into each feature, starting with [Onboarding Setup](../onboarding/01-onboarding-setup.md).
